01 — The PantryGarde manger

A full fridge, an empty plan, one list that knows.

pAIntry starts where most grocery runs go wrong: at a fridge nobody has really looked into. Text a photo of it, or of a receipt, and the pantry fills itself with items, quantities and expiry dates. You confirm them in one tap.

From there the kitchen does the thinking. Nutrition gaps turn into a shopping list, the list is priced across three stores, and every reason on every card links back to the data it came from. The code does the math; the model only writes the words.
